WHY p_003 CAN NOW BE ASSOCIATED WITH PATIENT 3
The original audit's pixel hashes link:
  video_4 -> p_003_video_1: 233 matching image groups.
  video_5 -> p_003_video_2: 160 matching image groups.
  video_6 -> p_003_video_3: 112 matching image groups.
Total: 505 matches, covering all 505 current p_003 test images.

Table I assigns all three source videos to Patient 3. Therefore the chain is:
  p_003 test image = matching Video 4/5/6 image -> Patient 3.
This supports a confident Patient 3 association for the whole p_003 test group,
not merely an inference from the digits 003. The matching source copies were
in the original train/validation data, but those copies are excluded from the
CURRENT processed train/validation sets.

CURRENT PATIENT OVERLAP AND LEAKAGE ASSESSMENT
Confirmed patient assignments by split are disjoint:
  Train {Patient 2}; validation {Patient 4}; identified test portion {Patient 3}.
Thus there is NO CONFIRMED patient overlap among current processed splits.
The previously checked current manifests also have no shared known source
components or exact original-image pixel hashes across splits.

This does not establish zero possible patient leakage for all 966 test images.
The p001 and p006 groups could only be declared different patients from 2 and 4
once their code-to-publication-patient mapping is verified. Their names suggest
IDs 001 and 006, but suggestion alone is not sufficient evidence.
The existing manifest labels patient_001 and patient_006 were derived from
filenames; those generated labels do not independently verify patient identity.

The identified portion of the current partition is now supported as
patient-separated: Patient 2 for training, Patient 4 for validation, Patient 3
for part of testing. Complete test-set patient separation remains unresolved
only with respect to the unverified groups. Exact-match absence cannot by
itself exclude different recordings from the same patient.

Each of training and validation contains only one known patient. Consequently,
hundreds of frames do not mean hundreds of independent patients, and validation
performance remains based on a very small patient sample even without overlap.

ORIGINAL DATASET VERSUS CURRENT PROCESSED DATA
The original splits mixed frames from the same videos across train/validation.
With the supplied mapping, the original train and validation each included
Patients 2, 3, and 4. Exact copies of Patient 3 images also appeared in test.
That original partition had confirmed patient overlap and duplicate leakage.
It is not the partition used by the saved baseline model.
The current processed data retains only Patient 2 in train and Patient 4 in
validation, and keeps the 505 Patient 3 images solely in test.
